# Tattrex reference study

The requested references were opened in the isolated Playwright browser. Screenshots are saved beside this note. These are references for composition and interaction; their imagery, branding and copy are not being used in Tattrex.

| Reference | What was observed | Application to Tattrex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Ezera](https://ezeratech.ae/) | Warm neutral field, restrained oversized type. Only the text-led fallback rendered in this browser; the intended animated experience was not verified. | A confident light surface and quieter supporting copy. No motion conclusions inferred from the fallback. |
| [Lando Norris](https://landonorris.com/) | Central portrait and changing helmet layers; oversized expressive typography; image compositions vary in scale and position as the story progresses. | A scientific object as the stage, with typography composed around it. Alternate immersive and editorial sections instead of repeating one grid. |
| [FMI](https://www.fmi-industries.com/) | Full-viewport cinematic opening; large type anchored low; quiet navigation; imagery becomes the environment for application navigation. | Full-frame research scenes and a clearer separation between opening impact and technical explanation. |
| [LinusBio](https://www.linusbio.com/) | Large scientific positioning; moving biological imagery; spacious science explanations with expandable topics. | Keep the science legible. Couple each research topic to a focused visual, without adopting another company's evidence or clinical claims. |
| [Abdul Kalam](https://param.design/abdulkalam/) | Particle-based portrait, scroll-led text changes, persistent chapter rail and a narrative progression. | A persistent chapter index and a three-part scene sequence: See, Work, Discover. Build a storyboard for transitions, not just a long screenshot. |
| [Tour Kyrgyzstan](https://tour-kyrgyzstan.com/) | Immersive film, edge rulers and restrained technical labels; asymmetric placement of headline, supporting copy and CTA. | Small imaging coordinates, frame edges and chapter marks, grounded in the radiology context. Keep the interface readable and the next step visible. |
| [Aquirin](https://aquirin.com/SR) | A physical-looking scrolling instrument with deliberate transport controls and red accents; a sparse surrounding canvas. The root page first appeared mostly empty, while SR loaded the instrument. | Make voice interaction feel tangible through a compact command instrument. Use clear familiar controls and avoid reproducing the reference device. |

## Direction for Concept 03

**A living imaging atlas.** A brighter, sculptural opening leads into a three-chapter story. The visual object changes role: a study to examine, a workspace to operate, and a research field to explore. The page alternates large scenes with short editorial explanations.

Retain the dot-free Tattrex logo, single Manrope family, semantic design tokens, warm paper, charcoal and vermilion. Give Platform and Labs equal conversion paths. Use original generated artwork and native editable Figma UI.

Provide desktop, tablet and mobile layouts; a linked three-scene storyboard; focused research panels; and responsive QA. Actual scroll-linked WebGL and production accessibility behaviours belong to implementation after the design review. Do not make product availability, regulatory clearance or performance claims from these references.
