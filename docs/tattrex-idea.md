# Tattrex — Radiology, shaped by you

> A voice and AI native platform for radiologists. A research partner for what comes next.

## The idea

Tattrex brings together two connected ambitions: helping radiologists work more naturally in their daily practice, and helping them turn clinical insights into useful imaging tools.

Radiologists shape both the workflow and the research. Tattrex works alongside them to build the technology, develop the evidence, and carry promising ideas toward regulatory approval and clinical products.

## Tattrex Platform

### Stay with the study. Speak the next step.

The main product is a voice and AI native radiology platform that brings studies, prior examinations, image analysis and reporting into one workspace.

The goal is to let radiologists express what they want to do in natural language while keeping their attention on the images and their clinical reasoning. Voice becomes a way to navigate and act across the workflow, extending beyond dictation.

The platform vision includes:

- **Study prioritisation:** use AI assistance to surface cases that need attention and explain the suggested priority.
- **Image navigation:** change windows, views and reconstructions through natural-language instructions.
- **Imaging operations:** create maximum intensity projections (MIP) and multiplanar reconstructions (MPR) within the reading flow.
- **AI assistance:** highlight relevant abnormalities and keep findings connected to their location in the images.
- **Clinical context:** retrieve prior examinations and compare the same anatomical region across time.
- **Reporting:** carry reviewed findings into a structured draft that the radiologist can edit and finalise.
- **Personal workflows:** let radiologists shape routines, reporting preferences and clinical tools around their own practice.

### An example reading flow

1. **“Open the most critical patient.”** The platform identifies a priority study and explains why it was surfaced.
2. **“Change it to brain window.”** The images update to the requested window.
3. **“Highlight acute infarct.”** An abnormality overlay appears in the context of the scan.
4. **“Create MIP on 15 mm.”** The platform generates the requested projection.
5. **“Give me MPR.”** The study appears in axial, coronal and sagittal planes.
6. **“Pull up priors.”** Earlier imaging is brought into the workflow when available.
7. **“Compare the ventricular region across time.”** The corresponding anatomy is presented for comparison.
8. **“Draft the report.”** A structured draft brings the findings and impression together for radiologist review.

This is the workflow vision illustrated on the website. The website sequence is a scripted demonstration using source CT images and expert reference annotations; its prior comparison is illustrative.

## Tattrex Labs

### Your questions. A wider field.

Tattrex Labs helps individual radiologists bring clinical ideas about imaging to life. A collaboration can begin with an observation, a recurring problem or a question from practice. A finished research proposal is not required.

Labs works across three connected areas:

### Clinical ideas

Work with radiologists to define a clinical question, build a focused tool and test its usefulness. The radiologist contributes clinical insight and remains involved throughout development and validation.

### Foundation models for medical imaging

Develop models that learn useful representations from imaging data and can support tasks across modalities and anatomy. Each project has a defined dataset, intended use and validation scope.

### Novel imaging biomarkers

Explore imaging signals that may reveal new information about disease and outcomes. Begin with a testable clinical hypothesis and build evidence for the proposed biomarker.

### Every modality. The whole body.

The research ambition spans imaging modalities and the full body. Individual projects start with a specific clinical problem and a clear plan for data, development and validation.

## How we work with radiologists

### 1. Find the question

Start with a clinical observation or workflow need. Define the problem, intended use and value together.

### 2. Build the evidence

Develop and validate the technology with radiologists involved throughout. Test what matters in practice and refine the tool around clinical feedback.

### 3. Carry it through

Tattrex leads the path toward regulatory approval and productisation for the intended use. Availability, validation and regulatory status are assessed for each product and market.

## One connected direction

The platform supports everyday reading and reporting. Labs gives radiologists a way to pursue the clinical questions that emerge from that work. Together, they create a path from clinical insight to research, evidence and practical tools.

The current platform is at demo version 0.1. Its scope will expand through collaboration with radiologists and development of the broader platform and research vision.
